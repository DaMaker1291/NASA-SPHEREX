HOW TO SPOT MOTION

A tiny movie of a very large sky.

SPHEREx revisits the same strip of sky across its survey. Differences between visits can reveal proper motion, variable stars, or an object that moved between exposures.

01

Choose a patch

Use a named nearby star or enter any RA and Dec. A 0.30° field is a good starting point for a moving target.

02

Play the visits

Press Play sequence to cycle through real detector frames. Use the filmstrip to jump to any date.

03

Blink or subtract

Blink A / B toggles two visits. Difference subtracts them so changes in position or brightness become obvious.
