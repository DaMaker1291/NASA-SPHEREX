How to use this prototype

01

Prepare images

Ask the backend team for time-separated PNG cutouts of the same sky region, aligned to the same coordinate grid.

02

Load and play

Choose the PNGs, step through them, play the animation, and zoom or drag to investigate details.

03

Compare frames

Pick two observations and blink between them or show both together. Dates and wavelengths can be added when the backend supplies metadata.
