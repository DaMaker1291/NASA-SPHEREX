How to use this viewer

01

Load real frames

Choose a field and load real SPHEREx frames. The backend queries IRSA SIA2 and renders the actual IMAGE FITS pixels.

02

Load and play

Step through the real visits, play the timeline, and zoom or drag to inspect the detector cutouts.

03

Compare frames

Pick two real observations and blink between them or show both together. Dates and detector bands come from SIA2.
